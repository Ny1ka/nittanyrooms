For print, stickers and single-color use. The doorway becomes a cut-out and the door leaf stays solid.
Small sizes
64, 32 and 16 px. At 16 px the door leaf drops out and only the lit doorway stays.
The idea
A map pin holding a lit, half-open door: a room you can walk into. The yellow matches open rooms on the floor plans.
